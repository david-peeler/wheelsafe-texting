# WheelSafe fall-alert texts

WheelSafe Fall Alerts is a text-message program operated by University of Illinois as part of a research study on fall detection for wheelchair users. A study participant may invite you to be an emergency contact. If you join by texting JOIN, you get a text only when their watch detects a possible fall.

- [Terms & Conditions](terms-and-conditions.html)
- [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html)
- [How contacts opt in](sms-opt-in.html)

Reply STOP to any WheelSafe text to opt out, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. WheelSafe is not an emergency service: in an emergency, call 911.
